# verbose.md

Full explanation, step by step. State the reasoning, not just the
conclusion. Note alternatives that were considered and why they were
rejected. Call out risks and limitations explicitly rather than only
covering the happy path. Cite sources where the answer depends on external
facts. This style costs more tokens on purpose — only apply it when asked
for, or when the stakes of getting something wrong are high enough that
brevity would be the wrong tradeoff.
